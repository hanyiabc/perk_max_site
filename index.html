What it tracks

Built for the way perks actually work.

Recurring credits

Monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual credits with progress bars and one-tap "used" marking.

Spend-gated credits

See your progress toward spend thresholds that unlock benefits — like companion certificates at $30k or $60k.

Certificates & free nights

Track Hilton and Marriott free nights and Delta companion certificates — see what's unused and how much it's worth, with valuations you can tune.

Track Subscriptions

Map Netflix, Instacart, and more to the card that reimburses them, and keep an eye on billing dates.

Usage history

Review what you used and what you missed by card and period — and exactly how much value slipped away.

Your-cloud backup

Link to your own Google Drive to back up and sync your wallet. You hold the data — we never see it.

Portrait or landscape

Looks right held tall or wide.

The layout reflows to whatever you're on. Glanceable, scrollable progress on a phone in portrait — a dense, multi-column overview when you go landscape on a tablet or desktop. Same data, fit to the screen.

Private by design

Your wallet is yours.

PerkMax stores everything on your device and answers to no server we run. There is no PerkMax account, because there is no PerkMax backend to hold your data.

  • On-device first

    All your cards, credits, and history live in a local database on your phone or computer — not in the cloud by default.

  • No analytics, ever

    No telemetry, no trackers, no ad SDKs, and no third-party scripts. This app loads zero external resources.

  • No account required

    Open the app and start tracking. No sign-up, no email, no password — nothing to leak.

  • You own the backup

    The only network access is the cloud backup you explicitly turn on, writing to your own Google Drive.
