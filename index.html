What it tracks

Built for the way perks actually work.

  • Recurring credits

    Monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual credits with progress bars and one-tap "used" marking.

  • Spend-gated credits

    See your progress toward spend thresholds that unlock benefits — like companion certificates at $30k or $60k.

  • Certificates & free nights

    All-or-nothing tracking for Hilton and Marriott free nights and Delta companion certs — with valuations you can tune.

  • Subscriptions

    Map Netflix, Instacart, and more to the card that reimburses them, and keep an eye on billing dates.

  • Usage history

    Review what you used and what you missed by card and period — and exactly how much value slipped away.

  • Your-cloud backup

    Optional, conflict-safe sync to your own Google Drive or OneDrive. You hold the data — we never see it.

Private by design

Your wallet is yours.

PerkMax stores everything on your device and answers to no server we run. There is no PerkMax account, because there is no PerkMax backend to hold your data.

  • On-device first

    All your cards, credits, and history live in a local database on your phone or computer — not in the cloud by default.

  • No analytics, ever

    No telemetry, no trackers, no ad SDKs, and no third-party scripts. This very page loads zero external resources.

  • No account required

    Open the app and start tracking. No sign-up, no email, no password — nothing to leak.

  • You own the backup

    The only network access is the cloud backup you explicitly turn on, writing to your private Drive or OneDrive app folder.
